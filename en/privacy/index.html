Chagok Privacy Policy

한국어

HyunJung Lim (“we”) respects the privacy of everyone who uses the Chagok app (“the app”). We do not run our own servers and do not directly collect personal information such as your name or contact details. However, in the free version Google processes some device information to show ads, and the iPhone and iPad app sends usage statistics to Google Firebase to help us improve it. This policy explains what the app stores and where, and what information is processed for ads and usage statistics.

1. Information we collect

We do not directly collect any personal information, such as your name, email or phone number. The app has no sign-up or login. Information that Google processes for ads is described in section 3, and usage statistics in section 4.

2. What the app stores and where

  • Habits and records: The habits you create, your completion history and the stars you earn are stored on your device.
  • iCloud sync: If sync is on, this data is stored in your own private iCloud storage and synced across devices signed in to the same Apple ID. We cannot access it. You can turn sync off in the app’s Settings.
  • Widgets: Widgets read data through shared storage on the same device only.
  • Notifications: Habit reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent off the device.
  • Purchases: Premium purchases are handled by Apple’s App Store. We never receive your payment details or billing information. The app only checks whether a purchase is active.

3. Advertising and tracking

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. If you buy Premium, no ads are shown and the information below is not processed for ads. (Usage statistics follow section 4.)

Information processedAdvertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), IP address and the approximate location inferred from it, device information such as model and OS version, ad impressions and clicks, and app crash and performance data
PurposesServing ads, measuring ad performance, preventing invalid clicks, and personalized ads if you allow them
  • Tracking permission: The first time you open the app, it asks whether to allow tracking across other companies’ apps and websites. You can use every feature of the app the same way if you don’t allow it; you will see non-personalized ads. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on your device.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: The app first asks for your consent to use information for ads. You can change your choice at any time in the app’s Settings > Ad privacy settings.
  • Your habit data, such as habit names, completion history and stars, is never shared with ad companies.
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. Usage statistics (Google Firebase Analytics)

To learn which features are used and where the app falls short, the iPhone and iPad app sends usage statistics to Google Firebase Analytics. This applies to both the free and Premium versions. The Mac app does not send them.

Information processedApp usage events (number of habits, when habits are checked and for which day, time between visits, opening the app from notifications or widgets, which screen opened the Premium page, etc.), an app instance ID assigned by Firebase, device model, OS and app version, country and region inferred from your IP address, and the advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking)
Not processedAnything you type, such as habit names and notes
PurposesImproving the app, understanding how features are used, and measuring ad performance (free version)
How to opt outThe app has no separate setting to turn off usage statistics; deleting the app stops them. Anything you type, such as habit names and notes, is never sent. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, statistics are stored only if you consent to storing information on your device in the consent form, and you can change this choice in the app’s Settings > Ad privacy settings.

5. Sharing and third parties (including international transfer)

We do not sell or share personal information with third parties. For ads (section 3) and usage statistics (section 4), information is transferred abroad and processed as follows.

RecipientGoogle LLC (contact: support.google.com/policies)
CountryUnited States
When and howSent over the network when ads are loaded in the free version, and when usage statistics are sent while you use the app
Items and purposesAs described in sections 3 and 4
RetentionAds: governed by Google’s Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy). Usage statistics: up to 14 months after collection
How to opt outIf you don’t allow tracking, your information is not used for personalized ads. Buying Premium stops the transfer for ads. For usage statistics, see section 4.

iCloud and App Store payments are services between you and Apple, governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

6. Retention and deletion

Your data is kept until you delete it. Deleting habits in the app or deleting the app removes data from your device. To delete data stored in iCloud, go to Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage on your device. Retention of information processed by Google for ads and usage statistics is described in section 5.

7. Your rights

All habit data lives on your devices and in your iCloud, so you can view, edit or delete it in the app at any time. You can change your ad choices as described in section 3, and usage statistics are described in section 4. For questions, contact us at the address below.

8. Security

Data on your device is protected by iOS and macOS security features. Data in iCloud is protected by Apple’s encryption and account security.

9. Children’s privacy

The app is not directed at children under 14 (or under 13 in the United States), and we do not collect personal information from children. Ads are requested as general-audience ads, not child-directed ads.
