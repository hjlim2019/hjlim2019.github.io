FAQ

I bought Premium but it isn't unlocked on another device

Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID, then tap “Restore Purchase” on the Premium screen in Settings. Premium is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

Will I lose my records if I switch devices?

With Premium, turn on iCloud sync and your records are kept in your own iCloud, so they carry over even if you delete the app or get a new device. Without sync, records stay on that device only.

When do I get a star?

Every 3 completions in a row of a habit lights up a star. Stars gather into constellations as you fill in all 88.

I missed a day and broke my streak

For today and the past 2 days, long-press a habit and choose “Rest today” to keep your streak. A streak broken by a single missed day 3–7 days ago can be continued from “Broken streaks” on the Today tab.

I'm not getting reminders

Check that notifications are allowed in iPhone Settings › Notifications › Chagok. You can also check this from Settings › Notifications in the app.
